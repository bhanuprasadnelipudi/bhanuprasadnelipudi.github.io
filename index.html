How I think about design

A successful design is not the most elaborate one. Over-designing is a failure of its own.

At Bangalore Aircraft Industries, experienced scientists and engineers taught me to balance budget, material, strength, fit, usability and manufacturability, and to stop when the design is right instead of when it is impressive.
